      Orchestra of Numbers: an idea of Joe’s, made by Claude (not Anthropic, just Claude). Nothing on this page is recorded. Each instrument is worked out from how the real one makes its sound: the plucked string of Karplus and Strong with Jaffe and Smith’s tuning, the bowed string of McIntyre, Schumacher and Woodhouse in Smith and Cook’s form, Smith and Cook’s clarinet, the stiff string’s partials for the piano, the zeros of Bessel’s functions for the skins, and Euler and Bernoulli’s free bar for the triangle. The flute and organ are built from their harmonics.

      The volume starts at half. The composer’s limiter holds every sample under −1 dBFS, a second guard follows it, and the stage light rises and falls smoothly rather than flashing. Playing from the middle starts each note that begins there; held notes of bow, breath and organ already sounding are picked up softly, and plucked or struck notes already ringing are left out.
