# The tune leads

The ear found the first sketch upside down. In bars 1 to 3, the cello's long notes at mp carried 41 to 62% of what was heard, and the flute's tune, marked p, only 12 to 30%. Once the violin answered, in bars 4 to 7, the flute fell to 10 to 16%. On the last chord the cello took 60%.

So the flute now starts at mp and stands 3 dB forward in the mix, and the cello plays p and sits 4 dB back. A clarinet joins the tune from bar 5, a third or a sixth below it. The violin's answer in bars 5 to 7 comes down from mp to p, and a triangle rings once, very softly, on the last chord.

Heard again, the flute carries 39 to 67% of what is heard in every bar, and the cello 4 to 20%. Nobody playing is buried.
